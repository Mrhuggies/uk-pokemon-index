How the index works

Prices come from Cardmarket, Europe's main card marketplace, through the free TCGdex API. Each night the tracker takes the median of every card's Cardmarket trend price, 7-day average and 30-day average (the median stops one jumpy figure moving a card, which matters for vintage cards where listings mix conditions), converts it to pounds at the day's ECB euro reference rate, and stores it.

The index is chain-linked and equal-weighted: each day it moves by the geometric mean of every card's price change since the day before, so a £20 card counts as much as a £2,000 one. Each card's single-day move is capped at roughly +49% or −33% to stop thin listings swinging the index. It started at 1,000 on its first day, and the sub-indices split cards into Vintage (1999 to 2009), Modern (2010 to 2022) and Scarlet & Violet (2023 on).

Until there's enough nightly history, the board shows momentum: each group's 7-day average sale price against its 30-day average. Prices are for raw, ungraded cards and include the currency effect, so a weaker pound pushes the index up. For a UK sold-price check on any card, use its eBay UK link.
