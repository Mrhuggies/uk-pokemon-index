    How the index works

    Every night the tracker prices every English Pokémon card on TCGdex (about 20,000, TCG Pocket excluded) using Cardmarket, Europe's main card marketplace. Each card's price is the median of its Cardmarket trend price, 7-day average and 30-day average, converted to pounds at the day's ECB euro reference rate. The median stops one jumpy figure moving a card, which matters for vintage cards where listings mix conditions.

    The market index covers every card worth £3 or more on the previous night, so bulk commons don't swamp it. It is chain-linked and equal-weighted: each day it moves by the geometric mean of every card's price change since the night before, with each card's move capped at roughly +49% or −33%. It started at 1,000. Vintage is sets from 1999 to 2009, Modern 2010 to 2022, and Current era 2023 on (Scarlet & Violet, Mega Evolution). Blue chip is a fixed basket of 53 sought-after cards.

    Before the nightly history builds up, the dashed start of each line is an estimate: Cardmarket's 30-day and 7-day averages for the same cards, placed at the middle of each window. The tiles show momentum: each group's 7-day average sale price against its 30-day average. Prices are for raw, ungraded cards and include the currency effect, so a weaker pound pushes the index up. For a UK sold-price check on any card, use its eBay UK link.
